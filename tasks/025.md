# Task 025

## Goal

Add criterion benchmarks for the diff engine and the graph lane layout, and a check that fails CI when a measured time is past its performance budget. This is the diff and graph half of the task; the viewer and status-tree benchmarks wait for the UI.

## Backlog task number

25 in [the plan](../docs/PLAN.md#v01-backlog): "Benchmarks with performance budgets, failing CI on regression". Row 25 becomes "Core done".

- `Cargo.toml` (workspace dependency `criterion`), `Cargo.lock`
- `crates/diff/Cargo.toml`, `crates/diff/benches/diff.rs` (new)
- `crates/graph/Cargo.toml`, `crates/graph/benches/layout.rs` (new)
- `docs/perf-budgets.toml` (new): the ceilings

## PRD law numbers

None. This task guards the performance budgets in the PRD ("Large repos and performance budgets") and the 200 ms diff budget in `docs/spec/diff-engine.md`.

## Constraints

- `criterion` is in the PRD's build-vs-library table. It is a dev-dependency only, with default features off (no rayon, no plotters).
- Inputs are generated inside the benchmarks from fixed seeds. Nothing reads a real repository. The Linux kernel benchmarks stay optional and out of CI.
- Criterion does not fail on a regression, so the check is ours: absolute ceilings from the PRD, not recorded baselines, in a hand-parsed file. No new dependency.
- The `diff` and `graph` crates stay pure; benchmarks live in `benches/`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `cargo bench -p diff -p graph` runs: 50k-line diff with about 2k changed lines (Myers and histogram), hunk building, word diff of changed pairs, the 10k-line open case, the worst cases (all lines changed, many repeated lines, a reversed file), and the graph layout of 100k-commit histories (linear, merging, wide, octopus), a page, and a 60-row window.
- `cargo xtask bench-check` fails when a median is past its ceiling, when a benchmark has no ceiling, and when a ceiling has no benchmark. Its parsing and comparison have unit tests, each shown to fail on a planted bug.
- Shown to fail on a planted slowdown in the diff crate and in the graph crate.
- CI runs the check.
- `cargo xtask check` passes.

## Out of scope

- The diff viewer first-paint benchmark and the status-tree benchmarks (they need the UI).
- The optional Linux kernel real-repo benchmarks.
- Kani proofs and the Quint models.

## UI half (branch `task-025-benches-ui`)

The rest of the task: the benchmarks that needed the UI crate, their ceilings, and the check that runs them.

- `crates/ui/benches/ui.rs` (new): `tree_rows` for a status of 100k and of 2k changed files, and the diff viewer's rows (`ui::diff_rows::rows`, split and unified) for a 10k-line diff and for a 10k-line file in which every line changed. `crates/diff/benches/diff.rs`: building the patch for one hunk and for three lines of a hunk of the 50k-line diff (the pure part of "stage a hunk to UI updated").
- `docs/perf-budgets.toml`: ceilings for the seven new benchmarks, each with its source (PRD "status refresh ... 100k-file repo < 100 ms", "stage a hunk ... < 16 ms", the frame, the 50 ms first-lines budget).
- `cargo xtask bench-check --ui` also runs the `ui` benchmarks (GPUI must build); without the flag the `ui/` ceilings are not asked for.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: a Windows job, `Benchmarks (windows-latest, ui)`.
- `criterion` is already a dev-dependency (task 25, first half); `ui` gets it too. No new dependency.
- Acceptance: all 24 benchmarks within their ceilings on this laptop; the check shown to fail on a planted slowdown in the UI crate; `cargo xtask check`.
